Privacy Policy for Darkness BBS

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled when you use Darkness BBS.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal personal information required to provide the game service:

  • Username (Handle): To identify your character in the game.
  • Password: Stored as a secure hash (we never see your actual password).
  • Game Data: Progression, inventory, stats, and logs of in-game actions/chat.
  • Session Data: Standard cookies are used to keep you logged into the terminal.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To allow you to log in and save your progress.
  • To display your character to other players (via WHO or LOOK commands).
  • To maintain leaderboards (TOP command).
  • To facilitate in-game communication (SAY and BROADCAST).

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. All data remains within the project database.

4. Data Security

We implement standard security measures to maintain the safety of your information.

5. Your Rights & Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data. Please contact darkworkllc@gmail.com to initiate this process.

6. Legal Disclaimer

This software is provided for educational and entertainment purposes only, "as is" with no guarantees, no warranty of any kind, and no liability for anything.

7. Contact

For questions or support, contact darkworkllc@gmail.com.
