## 1. Introduction

**Important Note on Data Storage:** All data collected, processed, and stored by Battle Chess remains exclusively on your device. Nothing is stored on our servers or any external location other than your own device. We do not operate any servers for data storage or processing of user information. **No data is saved outside of the phone.**

Battle Chess ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile game. All operations are performed locally on your device with no data transmission to external servers.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

Since all data remains on your device, we do not collect any personal information from you. The application processes the following types of data locally:

### Game Progress & Learning
- Win/loss records
- AI Learning Database (move weights and tactical experience)
- Difficulty levels unlocked
- Custom settings and preferences

### Device Information
- Device model and manufacturer
- OS version
- Hardware specifications (for performance optimisation)

### Local Multiplayer (LAN)
- Local IP address (only when hosting or joining a LAN game)
- Device name (as broadcasted to other players on the same network)

---

## 3. How We Use Information

All data is used exclusively for the following purposes within the application on your device:

### Gameplay Functionality
- Manage game state and chess rules
- Run the AI engine (Minimax)
- Facilitate local network multiplayer matches
- Provide high-impact combat animations and effects

### User Experience
- Save game settings
- Track personal achievements and history
- Optimise performance for your specific hardware

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage Only
- **All data is stored exclusively on your device**
- No data is transmitted to external servers
- No cloud backup or sync functionality

### Data Deletion
