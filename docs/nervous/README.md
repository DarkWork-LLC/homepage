**Nervous?** is a state-of-the-art Flutter mobile application developed by **Dark Work, LLC LLC**. It measures real-time nervousness, anxiety, and physiological stress using phone sensors (accelerometer/gyroscope tremor analysis) and paired smartwatch biometric telemetry (Heart Rate, HRV, Skin Temperature).

**🔒 100% Local Processing**: No third parties and no data ever leaves your phone. All telemetry, audio recordings, and session history are stored securely on-device using local SQLite storage.

## Best Practices: How to Hold the Phone
- Hold the phone upright in your hand or rest your hand lightly on a flat surface (desk/table).
- Keep your hand relaxed to allow sensors to capture micro-tremors without arm movement interference.

---

## Key Features
- **Standalone or Companion Mode**: Works with or without a paired smartwatch.
- **Nervous? Dashboard & Interactive Cards**
- **Flame-Powered Polygraph / Lie Detector**:
  - **Flexible Session Durations**: Pick 1, 2, 5-minute intervals, or manual/unlimited recording.
  - **Audio Recording & Playback**: Record session audio (with mandatory legal consent confirmation) and replay recordings.
  - **AI Speech Variance & Lie Analysis**: Analyze audio recordings for vocal tremor, energy variance, pitch jitter, and nervous speech to determine potential lie moments.
  - **Saved Reports & Full Review**: Review saved reports (up to 5 reports maximum stored in SQLite with automatic FIFO cleanup deleting the oldest first).
- **Smartwatch Companion & Sensitivity Calibration**
