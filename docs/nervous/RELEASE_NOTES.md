# Google Play Store Release Notes - Nervous App (v1.0.0+1)

## What's New in This Release

We are thrilled to release the latest update for **Nervous**, featuring advanced lie detector session management, AI voice analysis, and optimized local SQLite storage!

### Key Features & Updates:
- **🎙️ Flexible Lie Detector Session Durations**: Choose between 1-minute, 2-minute, 5-minute, or manual/unlimited session recording intervals.
- **🤖 AI Speech Variance & Lie Analysis**: Listen to and process recorded audio sessions to detect moments of nervous speech, vocal tremor, and potential deception based on acoustic pitch jitter and energy variance.
- **📂 Saved Reports Review & Audio Replay**: Tapping any saved report now opens a detailed review screen allowing full viewing of metrics, variation timings, and voice recording replay.
- **🗄️ SQLite Storage & Automatic Space Management**: All reports and voice recordings are stored securely 100% on-device in SQLite, with an automatic storage limit enforcing retention of the last 5 reports (deleting oldest first).
- **⌚ Real-Time Sensor & Watch Monitoring**: Live tremor tracking and wearable heart rate synchronization.
