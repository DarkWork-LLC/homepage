## 1. Best Practices & How to Hold the Phone
To ensure accurate sensor readings and tremor detection:
- **Phone Placement**: Hold the phone upright in your hand or rest your hand lightly on a flat, stable surface (such as a desk or table).
- **Relaxation**: Keep your hand and arm relaxed. Avoid deliberate shaking or excessive hand movement so the accelerometer and gyroscope can isolate involuntary micro-tremors and autonomic stress indicators.

## 2. Operating Modes: With or Without a Smartwatch
Nervous? works in two modes:
- **Standalone Mode (No Watch Required)**: Relies entirely on the phone's 3-axis accelerometer and gyroscope.
- **Enhanced Companion Mode (With Smartwatch Paired)**: Unlocks continuous Heart Rate (BPM), HRV, and skin temperature.

## 3. Dashboard Tab
- **Nervousness Index**: Real-time stress score (0-100%) and category (*Calm*, *Mild Anxiety*, *Nervous*, *High Stress*).
- **Interactive Metric Cards**: Tap any card (Hand Tremor, Heart Rate, HRV, Skin Temp) for detailed explanations.

## 4. Lie Detector Tab (Flame Engine & Final Reports)
- **Animated Neural Scan**: Features an animated neural network background (`NeuralScanGame`).
- **Baseline Calibration**: Tap **Start Lie Detector Test** and remain calm for the 20-second baseline countdown.
- **Final Report**: When finished, tap **Finish & View Report** to review full timings, baseline statistics, max stress deltas, truth/deception breakdown, and disclaimers.
